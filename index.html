Privacy Policy for TAISTIGO

Effective Date: October 2026

TAISTIGO ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when using our multi-role platform across Consumer, Vendor, and Rider accounts.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile picture, and authentication credentials via manual registration, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In.
  • Role-Specific Details:
    • Consumers: Delivery addresses and transaction histories.
    • Vendors: Restaurant profiles, menu configurations, and business contact data.
    • Riders: Vehicle details, operational statuses, and live location data for active deliveries.
  • Technical & App Usage Data: IP address, device identifiers, and platform activity logs.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your user account and grant role-appropriate access permissions (`Consumer`, `Vendor`, or `Rider`).
  • To process transactions, manage orders, and facilitate delivery operations.
  • To communicate transaction updates, support responses, and service announcements.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share minimal necessary information between Consumers, Vendors, and Riders strictly to fulfill active orders (e.g., sharing delivery addresses with assigned riders). We do not sell or rent user data. Data is processed through secure cloud infrastructure providers including Supabase, Google Cloud, and integrated payment processors.

4. Account Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request account deletion or data removal at any time by contacting our support team.

5. Contact Us

For questions or privacy concerns, contact support at: taisttigo@gmail.com
